# Privacy Policy (Sample)
Last updated: 2025-08-26

This app is for entertainment and self-reflection only.

## Data We Collect
- **In-app data**: Reading history (questions, spreads, timestamps) is stored **locally on your device** by default.
- **Diagnostics**: If you opt in, we may collect anonymized crash and performance data to improve stability.

## How We Use Data
- To provide core features (e.g., history).
- To improve the product (e.g., stability analytics).

## What We Don’t Do
- We do not sell your data.
- We do not track you across apps or websites without consent.

## Third-Party Services
If we later integrate ads, A/B testing, or cloud AI readings, we will disclose the provider, data types, and purposes in Settings and provide an opt-out.

## Your Choices
- Clear local history in Settings.
- Disable personalized/cloud readings (if enabled).

Questions? Email: support@example.com
